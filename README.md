# 👨‍💻 Coding Journey

My coding journey began during my college days, where I first started with C++, thanks to the foundation I had from my higher secondary education. Having that prior knowledge gave me a small head start, but what truly changed things was when I discovered Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA).I slowly fell in love with problem-solving and enrolled in a DSA course by GeeksforGeeks, where I practiced consistently and built a strong foundation. This not only boosted my confidence but also played a major role in helping me land job opportunities.As I grew more comfortable with concepts, I switched to Java, which became my primary language for solving problems on platforms like LeetCode and Codeforces. At the same time, my curiosity pushed me beyond algorithms — I wanted to see my ideas come alive on the web.That’s when I picked up the MERN stack, learning how to build full-stack web applications. From designing user-friendly interfaces to working with backend logic and databases, it opened up a whole new side of development for me.Today, I’m balancing both worlds — practicing coding and DSA to strengthen problem-solving skills, while also building projects with the MERN stack to bring ideas to life. This blend of competitive programming and real-world development is shaping me into a well-rounded developer, constantly learning and growing every day.
